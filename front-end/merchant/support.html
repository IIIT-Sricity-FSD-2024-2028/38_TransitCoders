Frequently Asked Questions

How do I change my payout schedule?

You can change your payout schedule in Account Settings > Billing. We support weekly, bi-weekly, and monthly payouts for all our merchant partners.

Where can I find my API keys?

Your API keys are located under Settings > Developer Options. Ensure you keep these keys secure and do not share them in public repositories.

What carriers are supported in my region?

We have integrations with major local and international carriers like FedEx, DHL, BlueDart, and more. Use the Courier Selector in the shipment creation flow to see active carriers in your area.
