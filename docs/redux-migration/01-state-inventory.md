## 2. Structural State Findings & Pathologies

### A. Prop Drilling & Context Over-Coupling
- **`AgentLayout` God-Context (`features/agent/AgentLayout.jsx:163-167`):** Passes a 13-key object (`orders, user, agent, savingAvailability, saveAvailability, saveProfile, busyOrder, refreshOrders, setAcceptModalOrder, setConfirmModalOrder, advanceOrder`) down through React Router `<Outlet context>` to 5 child pages, and down again into `OrderCard` (`Accept`, `Verify`, `Advance`, `busy`). Any state change in `AgentLayout` (like `busyOrder` or `notice`) forces the layout and active child route to re-render.
- **2–3 level prop callbacks for refreshes:** `FinancialAnalytics` → `ExpenseView` (`onRefresh`), `UserManagement` → `AddOrEditUserModal` (`onSuccess`), `SystemConfiguration` → `AddOrEditDeliveryZone` (`onSuccess`), Hub pages → 3 modals (`onSuccess={refreshOrders}`).

### B. Duplicated & Out-of-Sync State
1. **User session read from two sources:** `RequireRole.jsx:15-18` fetches `API.auth.me()` and updates `localStorage`; `AgentLayout.jsx:49` captures `useState(getSessionUser)` on mount, whereas `AdminLayout`, `CustomerLayout`, `MerchantLayout`, `HubManagerLayout`, `Warehouse`, `OrderMessages`, `UserManagement`, `SystemConfiguration`, and `Reports` read `getSessionUser()` directly during render.
2. **Repeated fetching of the exact same collections across portal tabs (zero cache):**
   - `GET /orders` is fetched independently in `MerchantDashboard`, `MerchantOrders`, `TrackShipment`, `AnalyticsReport` (4 separate mounts), and in `useHubOrders` (4 pages) + `Warehouse` + `Reports` (6 separate mounts), plus `CustomerDashboard` + `CustomerOrders` + `CustomerTracking` (uses `getById`).
   - `GET /users` is fetched separately in `AdminDashboard`, `UserManagement`, `MerchantTickets`, and `ReportsAnalytics`.
   - `GET /hubs` is fetched separately in `AdminDashboard`, `UserManagement`, `SystemConfiguration`, `BookShipment`, `TransferHubModal`.
   - `GET /complaints` and `GET /feedback` are fetched separately in `AdminDashboard`, `ComplaintsManagement`, `FeedbackReview`, and `ReportsAnalytics`.
3. **Mutate-then-staleness across tabs:** Updating pricing in `PricingConfiguration` or hubs in `SystemConfiguration` or booking an order in `BookShipment` leaves other pages stale until a full unmount/remount triggers a new `useEffect` fetch.
4. **Inconsistent order normalization:** The same backend `IOrder` object is transformed into **three incompatible shapes** in the frontend:
   - Raw backend shape (`HubManager` pages, `AdminDashboard`)
   - `normalizeOrder` (`services/customerService.js:8-23`: maps `status` via `statusLabel()`, renames `id` → `orderId`, `backendId` → `id`)
   - `normalizeBackendOrder` (`services/merchantHelpers.js:6-36`: uppercases status with underscores `IN_TRANSIT`, renames `trackingId` → `trackingNumber`, `amount` → `price`, `weight` → `packageWeight`)
   - `mapOrder` (`features/agent/AgentLayout.jsx:18-46`: maps status to lowercase `'pending' | 'in-transit' | 'delivered'`, keeps `backendStatus`, renames `id` → `rawId`, formats `amount` as `'₹...'` string!)
5. **Inconsistent business rule copy (`DashBoard.jsx` vs `warehouseWorkflow.js`):** `DashBoard.jsx:144-165` duplicates inline `onAssign`/`onTransfer` checks (`order.pickupHubId === hubId && order.status === 'Pending' && !order.assignedAgentId`) instead of `canAssign(order, hubId)` / `canTransfer(order, hubId)` used by `OrderQueue`, `Dispatch`, and `HubTransfer`—allowing the `Assign Agent` / `Transfer Hub` buttons on the Hub Dashboard to appear even when warehouse check-in/check-out preconditions are not met.

### C. Wide Re-renders & Layout Remounting
- **`HubManagerLayout` unmounting on every route change (`App.jsx:76-83`):** Because `/hub/*` does not share a parent `<Route element={<HubManagerLayout />}>` with `<Outlet />`, navigating between `/hub/dashboard`, `/hub/queue`, `/hub/warehouse`, etc. tears down and recreates `Sidebar` and `Topbar`, resetting `Sidebar` `expanded` state and re-triggering `Topbar`'s `API.notifications.unreadCount()` request every single time.

### D. Race Conditions, Stale Closures & Uncancelled Requests
- **`AdminDashboard`, `UserManagement`, `ComplaintsManagement`, `FeedbackReview`, `ReportsAnalytics`:** Their `useEffect` fetchers have **no `active` flag and no `AbortController`**, unlike `RequireRole`, `BookShipment`, `CustomerDashboard`, and `AgentLayout`. Fast unmounts/navigations can `setState` on unmounted components.
- **`BookShipment.jsx:56-65`:** `API.pricing.quote` fires on every keystroke in `weight` (no debounce, no `AbortController`), relying on `quoteKey` matching to discard stale responses (which works for display, though rapid typing still floods the backend).
- **`Topbar.jsx:18-23`:** `unreadCount()` runs every 30s without checking `document.visibilityState` or aborting in-flight requests on unmount.
- **`OrderMessages.jsx:30-50`:** Well-guarded with `AbortController` and `version.current` ref—clean pattern.

### E. Persistence, Hydration & SSR
- **Persistence:** Strictly client-side (`localStorage` keys: `x-auth-token`, `user`, `swifttrack_remembered_email`; `session.js:3-36`, `Login.jsx:12-15,32-36`). `clearSession` also removes legacy `userRole` (`session.js:34`).
- **SSR/SSG:** Not applicable (pure Vite CSR SPA).
- **Existing Redux-like code:** None. `AgentLayout` (`Outlet` context) and `session.js` (`subscribeToSession` custom event) are the closest ad-hoc patterns. Note that `front-end/js/api.js:83-105` references `window.AppState` (legacy vanilla JS store, unused by `frontend-react/`).
